Each round
Children hear a letter's name and sound, find the picture that starts with it, then celebrate. X is the exception: fox ends with x.
Collections
Signed-in collections use saved correct answers across all games. Play without saving lasts for this visit.
Privacy
Signed-in progress and child settings sync to their account. Play without saving uses game defaults and keeps no progress.
Shortcuts
In a world, Esc goes back to its home and arrow keys move between letters.
