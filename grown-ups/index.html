    09About

    Each round
    Children hear a letter's name and sound, find the picture that starts with it, then celebrate. X is the exception: fox ends with x.
    Collections
    When a child is signed in, stamps and stars are theirs to keep on every device. Play without saving keeps them for one visit.
    Stickers
    Children earn stickers for first sounds, letter sets, rounds and finishing a world. They show on the home page and in each world.
    What's saved
    Each child's name, picture, settings, finished rounds (letter, world, misses) and stickers, in this game's Supabase project. A copy stays on devices they used, for offline play.
    Shortcuts
    In a world, Esc goes back to its home and arrow keys move between letters.

    Saved

    Reset these settings?

    Sound, letters, help, motion and worlds go back to how they started for this person. This can't be undone.
