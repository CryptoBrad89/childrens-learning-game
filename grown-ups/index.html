05About the game

Each round
Children hear a letter's name and sound, find the picture that starts with it, then celebrate. X is the exception: fox ends with x.
Collections
A signed-in player keeps each sound they get right, in every world. Play without saving lasts for that visit only.
Privacy
Nothing about the child is recorded or sent anywhere. These settings stay in this browser on this device.
Shortcuts
In a world, Esc goes back to its home and arrow keys move between letters.
