# Letters with Lucy word pictures (separate art job, no game changes)

Approved by Bradley 2026-10-23 8:58 PM CT. Nine real game words (from the letters list in
codex/home-flow-ribbon v1-letter-post/app.js), 3 per layout. Both the Codex set and the
ElevenLabs set use these same 9 words so they can be compared side by side.

Shared rules: ages 3-4, one clear object per picture, centered, no text/letters/numbers in
the picture, no Lucy character, friendly but not a generic flat clip-art face. Each picture
must sit on its layout's background without clashing.

## v1 Letter Post: apple, kite, van
Mood: vintage air-mail post office, stamps, postmarks, paper and cardboard, handmade.
Palette: cream paper #faf2df / #fffaf0, navy ink #253f65, post-office red #d34939,
air-mail blue #b9dce6, warm card #fff8e5. Candy-post theme adds pink #f9dfed and berry #a52766.
Style: picture looks like a charming illustrated postage stamp / letterpress print, with
navy linework, soft paper grain, limited red-navy-blue-cream palette, a little scalloped
stamp or postmark feel.

## v3 Moonboat: moon, octopus, fox
Mood: calm night voyage on a little boat under the stars, sleepy and dreamy.
Palette: deep night navy #191d39 / #272c4b, moonlight cream #f7f0e8, peach #f2b294,
periwinkle #9da7d9, plum dusk #30223e with gold #edc685, mint glow #8ce1e5.
Style: soft glowing storybook illustration, gentle gradients, moonlit rim light, tiny
stars and sparkles, rounded cozy shapes, readable on both a dark navy and a pale cloud background.

## v4 Ribbon Road: rabbit, umbrella, ball
Mood: bright, playful, candy-sweet ribbon trail, sparkles and beads.
Palette: pink base #ffd2eb, magenta #b41468 / #ee6bbd, turquoise #61ead7, sunny
yellow #ffb83f, paper #fff5fb, plum ink #432045. Purple theme #653bce / #e3d4ff, yellow theme #fff1a6 / #235fe9.
Style: glossy, chunky, toy-like 3D-ish illustration with soft shine, a ribbon or bow accent
where natural, little sparkle stars, bold friendly shapes.
